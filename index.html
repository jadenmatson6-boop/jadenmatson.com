How do I find out what my San Diego apartment building is worth?

Small apartment buildings are valued mainly on income: net operating income (rents minus operating expenses) divided by a market cap rate, cross-checked against price per unit and price per square foot from recent nearby sales. A multifamily broker can prepare a broker opinion of value using your rent roll and operating statement.

Does rent control apply to apartment buildings in San Diego?

California's Tenant Protection Act (AB 1482) caps annual rent increases at 5% plus local CPI, with a 10% maximum, for most multifamily buildings older than 15 years, and adds just-cause eviction rules. San Diego also has local tenant protection ordinances. Exemptions and details vary by property, so confirm how the rules apply to a specific building.
